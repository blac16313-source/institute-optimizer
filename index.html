Institute Settings

Basic scheduling rules for the prototype.

How the replacement engine works

Candidates are checked in this order.

1. Subject
Teacher must be qualified for the required subject.
2. Availability
Teacher must not already have a class in that period.
3. Workload
Teacher should remain within the maximum lecture limit.
4. Building
Same-building teachers are preferred.
5. Movement
Cross-building teachers are allowed when necessary.
6. Failure
If nobody qualifies, the period remains unassigned.
